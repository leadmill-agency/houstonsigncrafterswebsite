Earth Burger #20001

Project summary

Houston Sign Crafters proposes to coordinate, fabricate, permit, and install the complete signage package for Earth Burger #20001 at 4640 Scott Street in Houston. The scope is based on the ten-sheet signage drawing package dated June 30, 2026 and includes three illuminated exterior identity signs, drive-thru signage, wayfinding, a wall stencil, and three interior sign elements.

Our objective is to deliver the Houston location within the requested opening window while providing Sinelli Concepts with one local point of accountability for field verification, permitting coordination, fabrication, installation, and warranty support.

A practical path to installation

How the project moves

The sequence is built around Earth Burger's requested 90-day opening window. Agency review, approvals, and site readiness remain schedule dependencies.

1
Authorize. Execute the agreement and submit the 50% deposit.
2
Verify. Field survey and final dimensions, approximately 2-3 business days after site access.
3
Engineer and permit. Prepare required documents and submit after verified drawings. Review timing is controlled by third parties.
4
Fabricate. Approximately 4-6 weeks after final artwork, engineering, and permit approvals.
5
Install. Approximately 2-4 working days after fabrication, site readiness, and scheduling confirmation.
