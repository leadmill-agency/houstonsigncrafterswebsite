## Key takeaways

- **Channel letters** = individual lit 3D letters. Premium look, higher cost.
- **Cabinet/lightbox signs** = one lit box with a printed face. Budget-friendly, flexible.
- Many upscale Houston centers require channel letters in their sign criteria.
- Cabinet signs handle detailed logos and lots of text more easily.

## What's the difference between channel letters and cabinet signs?

**[Channel letters](/services/channel-letters)** are fabricated one letter at a time. Each letter is its own housing with LED lighting inside, mounted directly to your facade (or to a raceway). They can be front-lit, halo-lit, or both, and they read as a high-end, custom sign.

**Cabinet signs** (also called lightbox or box signs) are a single rectangular enclosure with a translucent face, usually printed or vinyl-applied with your name and logo, lit from inside. The whole sign is one unit rather than separate letters.

## What are the pros and cons of each?

**Channel letters**
- Pros: premium, modern look; even illumination; often required by nicer centers; great curb appeal.
- Cons: higher cost; best for shorter names and clean logos.

**Cabinet signs**
- Pros: lower cost; handle detailed logos and more text easily; quick to produce; easy to reface later.
- Cons: less upscale than channel letters; some landlords don't allow them.

## Which sign is better for a Houston storefront?

Neither is universally "better". It depends on your situation:

- **Choose channel letters** if you want a premium look, your landlord's sign criteria require them, or your name is short and bold.
- **Choose a cabinet sign** if you're budget-conscious, have a detailed logo or a lot of copy, or want something quick that's easy to update later.
