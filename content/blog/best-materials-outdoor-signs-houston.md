## Key takeaways

- Houston's sun, humidity, and storms demand UV-stable, rust-resistant materials.
- Aluminum/ACM, acrylic, and laminated cast vinyl are the workhorses for outdoor signs.
- For illuminated signs, outdoor-rated UL-listed LEDs are essential.
- Material choice always depends on the sign type and mounting location.

## Why does material choice matter so much in Houston?

Houston's climate is unusually tough on signage. Intense UV fades cheap inks and plastics, high humidity and rain corrode low-grade metal and hardware, and storm-season wind loads can stress anything that isn't engineered and mounted correctly. A sign that looks great for one season and fades, rusts, or peels the next is a false economy. The materials below are chosen specifically to hold up here.

## What are the best materials for outdoor signs?

**Aluminum and aluminum composite (ACM):** Rust-resistant, lightweight, and rigid, aluminum and ACM panels (like Dibond) are the standard for sign faces, backers, and monument panels. They won't corrode the way steel can in humid air.

**Acrylic:** For [channel letters](/services/channel-letters) and illuminated faces, acrylic gives clean color and even light diffusion. Paired with the right LEDs, it glows brightly day and night.

**Cast vinyl with laminate:** For [vehicle wraps](/services/vehicle-wraps), [wall and window graphics](/services/wall-graphics), and banner-style prints, premium cast vinyl with a UV-protective laminate resists fading and lasts years in the Texas sun, far longer than cheaper calendared vinyl.

**Powder-coated and stainless hardware:** The fasteners, raceways, and frames matter as much as the face. Powder-coated aluminum and stainless steel hardware resist the corrosion that humidity causes.

## What about illuminated signs?

For lit signs, the lighting is the part most likely to fail early if it's low quality. We use **UL-listed LEDs rated for outdoor/wet locations**, which run cooler, draw less power, and last far longer than older fluorescent or non-rated lighting. Good LEDs are why a well-built [monument sign](/services/monument-signs) or channel-letter set can stay bright for years with no maintenance.
