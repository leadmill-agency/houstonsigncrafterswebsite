- Permitting and installation should be included in the quote — ask if they aren't.

## What drives the cost of channel letters?

Several factors move the price up or down:

- **Size and letter count:** Bigger letters use more material and labor. A short business name in small letters costs far less than a long name in large letters.
- **Lighting style:** Front-lit letters are the most common. Halo-lit (reverse-lit) and combination front-and-halo letters cost more because of the added fabrication.
- **Mounting method:** Letters mounted flush to the wall differ in cost from a raceway- or backer-mounted set, which your landlord may require.
- **Permitting and installation:** A code-compliant [permit](/guides/sign-permitting-checklist) and licensed installation are part of a proper project. Make sure any quote includes them.
